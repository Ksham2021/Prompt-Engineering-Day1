# Reflection Report

## Introduction

This assignment helped me understand how prompt engineering plays an important role in improving AI-generated responses. I explored four different prompting techniques and observed how small changes in prompts significantly improved the quality of the outputs.

---

## What I Learned

### Zero-Shot Prompting

I learned that even without providing examples, giving clear instructions and constraints helps the AI generate more relevant responses.

### Few-Shot Prompting

Providing examples before asking the actual question improves consistency and helps the AI understand the expected output format.

### Chain-of-Thought Prompting

This technique encourages the AI to solve problems step by step, resulting in more logical and accurate responses.

### Role Prompting

Assigning a role such as teacher, software engineer, or marketing expert helps the AI generate responses that match the required expertise and tone.
