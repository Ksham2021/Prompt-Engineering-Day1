This repository contains my Day 1 Prompt Engineering assignment.

In this project, I explored four prompting techniques:

- Zero-Shot Prompting
- Few-Shot Prompting
- Chain-of-Thought Prompting
- Role Prompting

For each technique, I created five prompts and compared the outputs before and after improving the prompts.

This assignment helped me understand how providing context, examples, reasoning steps, and specific roles can significantly improve AI-generated responses.

The repository also includes screenshots, documentation, and a reflection report.
